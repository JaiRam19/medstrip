MedStrip Build Plan

Small steps, one design idea each. Do them in order. Anything else goes in the parking lot.

Drift check: three questions before you write code
  1. Which tag does this step have: system design, agentic design or setup? If what you are about to do has no tag, stop.
  2. Is it the next step shown above? If not, write the idea in the parking lot and come back.
  3. Can you say the design choice in two sentences? If not, answer the design question on paper first.

Every step follows the same loop

  1. DesignAnswer the design question on paper. 15 minutes.
  2. BuildWrite the smallest version that meets the requirements.
  3. Go liveWhen the step says so, switch that console screen from mock to your API.
  4. Break itRun the failure drill if the step has one.
  5. NoteWrite one line on what you learned. These become your interview stories.
